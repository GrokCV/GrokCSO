# DISTA-Net++ Experimental Results

This directory contains a model checkpoint and the accompanying training and evaluation logs for **DISTA-Net++**, provided for reference and further evaluation.

## Notes on the Released Results

The evaluation results for the provided checkpoint, as documented in the accompanying logs, are generally consistent with those reported in the paper. Some metrics, such as **PSNR**, show minor numerical differences, while most metrics remain comparable to or better than the reported values, including **CSO-mAP**, the primary evaluation metric. These differences do not affect the main conclusions of the paper.

The original corresponding logs from the experiments reported in the paper are no longer available. We therefore provide the logs currently available to us for reference and further evaluation.
We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause when comparing the released results with those reported in the paper and appreciate your understanding.
